Second-year Data Science and Artificial Intelligence student in Spain. I like working with real-world data, the kind that never arrives clean, and turning it into something people use every day.

What I'm building

A regulatory intelligence platform for law firms and consultancies. The code is private; this is what it does:

	•	Collects what more than a hundred official sources publish every day (websites, APIs and feeds), detects format changes and outages, and retries without missing a single publication, always respecting each site's rules and rate limits.
	•	Cleans and deduplicates documents, classifies them by topic and territory, and extracts deadlines from legal texts.
	•	Tracks each legislative initiative and alerts every team to what affects their clients.
	•	Includes a RAG chat that answers by citing the exact article with a link to the original, and says so when it can't find the answer.
	•	Runs on a cloud server, with daily data quality checks and thousands of automated tests.
